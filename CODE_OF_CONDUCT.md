# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Contributions Centre aims to support useful work and constructive review across different contribution types. Participation should be professional, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, code, artifacts, and decisions without personal attacks.
- State disagreement clearly and support technical claims with evidence where practical.
- Give actionable feedback tied to agreed acceptance criteria.
- Respect privacy; keep secrets, personal data, and private submission material out of public discussion.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and avoid manipulation of reviews, reputation, or bounty outcomes.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct inaccurate claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, malicious review manipulation, and retaliation against good-faith reports are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers should review reports and determine proportionate action, which may include warnings, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from participation. Report sensitive concerns privately to the project owner. Do not use this process to penalize good-faith technical criticism.

This policy is a project participation standard, not a claim that a formal enforcement body currently exists.
